Audio Loudness Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

How does K-weighting filter human hearing?

Per ITU-R BS.1770-4, raw audio passes through a two-stage digital filter: a high-shelf filter boosting frequencies above 1.5 kHz (modeling acoustic head diffraction) followed by a high-pass filter at 38 Hz (suppressing sub-bass inaudible loudness weight).

What happens if my track is louder than -14 LUFS?

Streaming services like Spotify and YouTube apply automatic volume normalization. If your track is -10 LUFS, Spotify turns your track down by 4 dB, which can cause excessive limiter squashing. Mastering to -14 LUFS preserves dynamic punch.

Why is True Peak (-1 dBTP) important?

When streaming services encode lossless WAV audio into lossy AAC or Ogg Vorbis formats, intersample reconstruction peaks can rise above 0 dBFS and cause harsh digital clipping distortion. Keeping True Peak under -1 dBTP ensures distortion-free playback.

Is this tool private?

Yes, 100%. Audio is decoded directly into browser memory using standard Web Audio APIs. Unreleased master tracks, vocals, and confidential podcasts never leave your machine.
